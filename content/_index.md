I'm a graphics programmer. I've worked on graphics professionally for years, and spent many more years on it in my free time before that. Honestly, it's a passion bordering on addiction.

I got into graphics through blog posts and talks from GDC, SIGGRAPH and other conferences. All of my excitement for it came from people who took the time to share what they had learned.

What I love most is efficient programming and solving the problems that come with tight constraints, like simulating a massive game world in real time at a stable frame rate. I love light transport and pretty images, and I think it's an art to write code that generates something beautiful.

I love the interaction between people: sharing ideas and helping each other, whether at conferences, on forums or through shared publications. I like reading and meeting people who work on graphics, and watching how the graphics programming space evolves. I already help people a lot on Discord, on forums and directly, and I noticed that I keep repeating myself. So I started this website to collect my ideas in one place, explain them more coherently, and hopefully help more people.

Thanks for stopping by, maybe one of my ideas is useful to you :)
